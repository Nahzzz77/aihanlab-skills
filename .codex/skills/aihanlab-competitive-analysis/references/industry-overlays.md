# Industry Overlays

Use the universal core for every analysis, then add only the overlay that materially changes the user's decision. Do not force every listed dimension into the report.

## Universal core

- Target user, buyer, job to be done, and switching trigger
- Acquisition, activation, time to value, and onboarding friction
- Core workflow, output quality, user control, and recovery
- Continued-use loop, retention mechanism, and habit or operational dependency
- Collaboration, integrations, pricing, monetization, and switching costs
- Trust, privacy, accessibility, compliance, reliability, and support
- Positioning claim, proof, strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats, and strategic implications

## AI products

Add dimensions such as:

- Grounding, factuality, provenance, and citation usability
- Prompt or workflow control, correction, regeneration, and human review
- Transparency, explainability, memory, personalization, and data boundaries
- Latency, reliability, context limits, model choice, and cost-to-quality tradeoffs
- Safety, evaluation evidence, abuse controls, and failure recovery

Ask whether the product's advantage comes from the model, proprietary context, workflow design, distribution, data flywheel, or switching cost.

## Travel products

Add dimensions such as:

- Preference matching, content freshness, source provenance, and recommendation diversity
- Itinerary feasibility, opening hours, geography, travel time, pace, and budget constraints
- Maps, booking handoff, collaboration, sharing, reminders, and in-trip utility
- Replanning, weather or disruption handling, offline access, location privacy, and traveler safety
- Authenticity and bias of social recommendations, sponsored content, and “avoid” claims

Separate inspiration from executable planning: a popular place is not automatically a feasible itinerary item.

## SaaS and enterprise software

Add dimensions such as:

- Roles, permissions, administration, auditability, reporting, and governance
- Integrations, APIs, data import/export, implementation effort, and migration
- Security controls, compliance evidence, data residency, support, and service reliability
- Workflow fit, customization, collaboration, adoption, and time to organization-wide value
- Packaging, seat economics, expansion path, procurement friction, and switching risk

Distinguish buyer requirements from daily end-user experience.

## Content platforms

Add dimensions such as:

- Supply health, creator onboarding, creation tools, and publishing friction
- Discovery quality, ranking controls, cold start, feedback signals, and search
- Creator incentives, analytics, monetization, distribution, and audience ownership
- Community loops, moderation, appeals, harassment controls, and trust
- Content rights, provenance, recommendation bias, advertiser influence, and safety

Evaluate both sides of the system: consumer value and creator or supplier sustainability.

## Marketplaces

Add dimensions such as:

- Supply density, demand quality, matching, liquidity, and geographic coverage
- Trust, identity, reviews, fraud, disputes, guarantees, and payments
- Fulfillment, service quality, cancellation, refunds, and exception recovery
- Take rate, incentives, disintermediation, multi-homing, and network effects
- Buyer and seller acquisition economics, retention, and cross-side dependencies

Avoid judging marketplace strength from interface polish without liquidity evidence.

## Consumer apps

Add dimensions such as:

- Onboarding, permission timing, first-session value, habit loop, and notifications
- Personalization, sharing, referrals, community, subscriptions, and cancellation
- Performance, accessibility, privacy, safety, device integration, and offline behavior
- Content or utility freshness, return triggers, and user control over algorithms

Treat engagement as valuable only when it supports the intended user outcome.

## Hardware and connected devices

Add dimensions such as:

- Setup, ergonomics, reliability, battery, repairability, durability, and accessibility
- Companion software, connectivity, compatibility, integrations, and ecosystem lock-in
- Privacy, local versus cloud processing, security updates, warranty, and support
- Manufacturing constraints, availability, total cost of ownership, and replacement cycle

Separate announced specifications from tested real-world performance.

## New or mixed categories

Derive an overlay instead of choosing the closest label mechanically:

1. List the user's highest-stakes jobs and failure costs.
2. Identify who chooses, pays, uses, supplies, administers, and bears risk.
3. List the switching criteria and the evidence each criterion needs.
4. Select 5–10 dimensions that could change the user's decision.
5. State which existing overlays were combined and why.

For multi-sided or AI-enabled products, combine overlays deliberately. An AI travel marketplace may require AI, travel, and marketplace dimensions, but only include rows that affect the decision.
